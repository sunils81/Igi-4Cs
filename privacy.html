Privacy Notice

How we use the information you give us in the IGI Diamond 4Cs Quiz and the IGI Colored Stone Quiz.

This notice explains what personal data the IGI gemology quizzes collect, why we collect it, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). If you took the quiz from outside India, the same rights are offered to you.

In short: we ask for your name, email, mobile number and a few details about you so we can show your result and talk to you about IGI courses. We only do that because you ticked the consent box. You can tell us to stop, and delete your data, at any time by writing to indiaeducation@igi.org.

1. Who is responsible for your data

International Gemological Institute (India) — IGI School of Gemology — is the Data Fiduciary for the information described here. Contact: indiaeducation@igi.org.

We do not ask for, and do not want, any government ID number, payment-card details, or any sensitive personal information. Please do not enter such details in free-text fields.

3. Why we collect it, and on what basis

We process this data on the basis of your consent, which you give by ticking the box before the quiz starts. We use it to:

  • verify your email address and show you your quiz result and feedback;
  • contact you by phone, email or WhatsApp about IGI gemology courses, including the course you enquired about;
  • understand which questions people find hard, so we can improve the quiz and our teaching.

We do not use your data for automated decision-making, and we do not sell it.

Your details are visible to IGI education counsellors and centre staff in India, strictly to follow up about courses. We also use these service providers, who process data on our behalf:

5. How long we keep it

We keep your quiz response and contact details for 24 months from your last interaction with us, after which they are deleted. If you enrol on an IGI course, your records are then kept under the student privacy notice given to you at enrolment. If you withdraw consent earlier, we delete your data as described below.

6. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may:

  • Access — ask what personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct — ask us to fix anything inaccurate or incomplete;
  • Erase — ask us to delete your data;
  • Withdraw consent — tell us to stop contacting you and stop processing your data;
  • Nominate — nominate another person to exercise these rights if you are unable to;
  • Complain — raise a grievance with us, and escalate to the Data Protection Board of India.

To do any of these, write to indiaeducation@igi.org. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it — one email is enough, and we do not ask you to explain why. Withdrawing will stop further contact about courses; it does not affect anything you have already enrolled in.

7. Grievance Officer

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data or your request, you can contact our Grievance Officer:

The Grievance Officer
International Gemological Institute (India)
Email: indiaeducation@igi.org

If you remain dissatisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Children

These quizzes are intended for people aged 18 and over. Under the DPDP Act, anyone under 18 is a child, and we are not permitted to process a child's personal data for marketing or to track them without verifiable consent from a parent or guardian. If you are under 18, please do not submit your details. If you believe a child has given us their details, write to indiaeducation@igi.org and we will delete them.

9. Cookies and tracking

The quiz pages use cookies and similar technologies for the Meta Pixel and Microsoft Clarity described above. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the quiz itself will still work.

10. Changes to this notice

If we change how we use your data, we will update this notice and the version below. Where the change is significant, we will ask for your consent again.
